Rekindled. How it works

Three acts

Everyone comes alone. That's the whole point.

BEFORE

Don't walk in blind.

Swipe events tagged by activity, vibe, and group size. Match into a chat 48 hours before — see the faces, ask the questions.

DURING

The activity carries you.

Concerts, classes, comedy, run clubs — sourced live from Eventbrite and Ticketmaster. The activity does the conversational heavy lifting; nobody has to invent the night.

AFTER

One hang plants the seed.

Group chat persists. Rekindled AI — an always-on host bot inside the chat — keeps it warm with prompts and playful hot takes. Follow-up hangs auto-suggest if engagement passes a threshold.
